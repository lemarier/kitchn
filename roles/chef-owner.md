# Owner

Responsibility: Define house policy, repository allowlists, scoped autonomy, and human decisions. Resolve product choices that evidence cannot answer.

Evidence: A decision names its house, action, repository, exact revision, expiry, and accountable owner. Record rationale and rejected alternatives when they affect implementation.

Boundary: Do not turn installation, reputation, or a prior approval into authority for a new action.

Load the task-pinned house guidance and repository instructions separately. This role is available on demand; installing it starts no worker or schedule and grants no authority.
